Mission & Vision

Why Ascofizz Exists

India has one of the world's highest rates of micronutrient deficiency — yet most supplement brands charge a premium that keeps quality nutrition out of reach for millions. We founded Ascofizz to break that cycle.

From our GMP-certified facility in Anand, Gujarat, we formulate, manufacture, and deliver premium nutraceuticals — effervescent tablets, spirulina, glutathione, biotin and more — at prices that make good health truly accessible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about Ascofizz products and services.

What makes effervescent vitamins different?⌄
Effervescent tablets dissolve in water before you drink them, which allows for faster absorption compared to regular swallowed tablets, and makes dosing gentler on the stomach.
Are Ascofizz products FSSAI approved?⌄
Yes. All Ascofizz products are manufactured in a WHO-GMP certified, FSSAI-licensed facility in Anand, Gujarat, and every batch is independently lab tested.
How long does delivery take?⌄
Orders are dispatched within 24–48 hours. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days across India; express delivery to major cities takes 1–2 business days.
Can I cancel or modify my subscription?⌄
Yes, you can pause, modify or cancel your Subscribe & Save plan anytime from your Account Dashboard with zero penalties.
What is your return policy?⌄
We accept returns on sealed, unused products within 7 days of delivery. Opened supplement packages cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?⌄
Yes, COD is available across India for a ₹60 charge, which is waived on orders above ₹599.
Are your supplements vegan?⌄
Several products, like Vita B12 Fizz, are 100% vegan. Check the product page's ingredients tab for details on each SKU.
Do you manufacture for other brands (B2B)?⌄
Yes — Ascofizz (via Ascovita Healthcare) offers private label and third-party manufacturing. Visit our B2B & Wholesale page to enquire.
