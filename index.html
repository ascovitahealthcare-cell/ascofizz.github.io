Our story

From contract manufacturing to our own shelf

For years we made supplements for other people's labels. We saw which formulas were honest and which were mostly marketing — and we got tired of watching good manufacturing get wrapped in bad products.

Ascofizz is what happens when the people who run the plant decide what goes in the tablet. Full disclosure on every active, dosed to RDA, in a format people actually enjoy taking.
