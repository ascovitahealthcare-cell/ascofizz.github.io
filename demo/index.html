How it is put together

The split that makes a brand swap a template change instead of a rebuild.

Core — shared by all sixWritten once, never forked
  • Authentication
  • Product model
  • Cart
  • Checkout
  • Payments
  • Orders
  • Customer accounts
  • Admin panel
  • Inventory logic
  • Search
  • Filtering & sort
  • APIs
Storefront — one per brandThe only thing a new tenant writes
  • Brand identity
  • Colour palette
  • Typography
  • Navigation
  • Homepage
  • Product cards
  • Product page
  • Category pages
  • Merchandising
  • Footer
  • Mobile nav
  • Motion

A brand pack is two files — template/brand.css and template/brand.js — declaring its palette, type, navigation, homepage, card, product page and portfolio. scripts/build-storefronts.py copies the engine and attaches the pack, publishing each storefront at /demo-1 … /demo-6. The live Ascofizz storefront keeps the site root and is untouched by any of it. Adding a seventh brand means writing a seventh pack; it does not mean touching the checkout.

White-label demonstration storefronts. Products, brands, prices and reviews in templates 01–05 are fictional and exist to show the platform.

Template 06 is the live Ascofizz storefront, included unchanged as the baseline.
